The Evidence Trap
Why fact-based conflicts never settle
The pattern
In political cases, the committee gathers evidence, rules, and moves on. The evidence phase clears within the first third of the timeline. In science and geopolitics, the evidence phase never fully releases. The argument loops back, demands more proof, and the case starves for a decision that facts alone cannot deliver.
What Wikipedia's last resort reveals
The Arbitration Committee is Wikipedia's court of last resort. Cases only reach it after every lower forum has failed. When those cases cluster around disputed facts rather than disputed conduct, they stay open longer because the committee cannot rule on what reality is. It can only manage behavior around the disagreement. The evidence trap is not a process failure. It is a signal that some conflicts are structurally beyond arbitration's reach.
