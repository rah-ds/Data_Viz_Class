Chromatic Personalities

Each audio feature is min-max rescaled across the 12 keys so that the lowest key maps to 0 and the highest to 1, amplifying relative differences. Keys are grouped by personality (highest z-score above the median, z > 1). The dashed grey polygon is the median of the rescaled values.
